How to Play AI Safety Connections

🎯 Objective

Normal: Find 4 groups of 4 concepts. Advanced: Find 3 groups of 4 (plus decoys). Each group corresponds to a category with increasing difficulty.

🕹️ Gameplay

  • Select 4 tiles that you think belong to the same category
  • Click Submit to check if your grouping is correct
  • Be careful! Many concepts can fit multiple categories
  • You have 4 attempts - use them wisely!

🎨 Difficulty Levels

  • EASY - More obvious connections
  • MEDIUM - Requires some AI safety knowledge
  • HARD - Subtle philosophical connections
  • HARDER - (Normal mode) Most challenging

⏱️ Timer & Score

  • The timer runs from the start of each game until you win or lose.
  • Score is based on correct categories, mistakes, and time. Higher is better!
  • Save your name when you win to appear on the Leaderboard (stored on this device).

📱 On Phones & Tablets

  • Tap a tile once to see its definition (when hints are ON). Tap again to select it for your group.
  • Use the Dictionary button for all definitions.

🐕 The AI Safety Dog

Your canine companion reacts to your progress:

  • 🐕 Happy - When you're doing well
  • 😟 Worried - When you make mistakes
  • 🎉 Celebrating - When you solve categories
  • 😴 Sleeping - When the game ends

💡 Tips

  • Hover (or tap on mobile) over concepts to see their definitions
  • Use the Dictionary for quick reference
  • Look for both technical and thematic connections
  • Some concepts might fit multiple plausible groups
  • Think about both the meaning and context of each term
